Privacy Policy for მართვის მოწმობა (Prava)

Last Updated: February 9, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to მართვის მოწმობა (Prava) ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile driving test application available on Android (and iOS) platforms.

The app is designed to run primarily on your device. Your test progress, preferences, and any local profile you create are stored locally on your device.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 On-Device Data

The app stores your data locally on your device. This data may include:

  • Test Progress: Your driving test results, scores, and completion history
  • App Preferences: Settings and preferences you configure within the app
  • Local Account Data: Any profile information (such as a display name or profile photo) you create, stored on your device

2.2 What We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or sell:

  • Personal information for advertising or marketing
  • Precise location data
  • Usage analytics or tracking identifiers
  • Data shared with third-party advertising networks

3. Data Storage

3.1 Local Storage

App data is stored locally on your device using the secure storage mechanisms provided by your operating system.

3.2 Your Control Over Your Data

  • Your data remains on your device
  • Uninstalling the app removes all associated data
  • You can clear app data through your device settings at any time

4. Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties for their own marketing or advertising purposes.

5. Camera and Photos

If you choose to set a profile photo, the app may request access to your camera or to a photo you select via the system photo picker. The selected image is stored locally on your device for use as your profile picture and is not used for any other purpose.

6. Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It is intended for users preparing for a driving theory test, subject to the appropriate age requirements in your jurisdiction.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page, and the "Last Updated" date above indicates when it was last revised.
